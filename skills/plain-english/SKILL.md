# Plain English

Use plain English from the first response through the rest of this chat.

- Use clear, everyday words and short, direct sentences.
- Apply this to chat responses, descriptions, comments, documentation, and abstraction names.
- Choose names that say what something is or does. Avoid vague or clever names.
- Avoid jargon and buzzwords when ordinary words say the same thing. When a technical term is needed for accuracy, use it and explain it briefly if the audience may not know it.
- Avoid overly abstract LLM word salad. Say what happened, what will happen, or what is needed in concrete terms.
- Keep the meaning precise. Plain English does not mean leaving out important detail.

This is a writing rule for work going forward. It is not a code or documentation audit and does not require inspecting or rewriting existing work unless the user asks for that.
